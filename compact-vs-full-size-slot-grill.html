Slot Grill Compact vs. Full Size: Which One?

Same tab-and-slot design, two sizes. The Compact is the Full Size at 80% scale. Both are thick carbon steel, made in Texas, set up by hand with no tools or hinges, burn charcoal or cooking wood, and flat-pack. The question is just how many people you cook for and how you travel.

What the numbers mean in practice

  • Cooking area: 146 sq in is sized for one to three people. 231 sq in is about 58% more grill, room for a family, cast iron alongside food, or big cuts.
  • Steel gauge: lower gauge number means thicker steel. The Full Size's 10-gauge is heavier than the Compact's 12-gauge; both are far thicker than typical stamped sheet-metal portable grills, and the Compact is still strong enough for cast iron.
  • Weight: about 14 lb vs. about 27.6 lb. The Compact is one-hand carry; the Full Size is a truck, UTV, boat or backyard grill.
  • Air gap: the space between the table and the bottom of the coal bed (2⅞" vs. 3⅝") helps minimize radiant heat to the surface below.
  • Packed: about 1" vs. about 1¼". Both slide into tight storage.

Pick the Compact if

  • You cook for one to three.
  • You want the lightest heavy-duty option.
  • Space matters: trunk, kayak, RV closet, camp box, job-site truck.
  • You'll carry it any distance from the vehicle.

Pick the Full Size if

  • You cook for a family or group.
  • You want room for cast iron and big cuts.
  • You travel by truck, UTV or boat, or it lives in the backyard.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between the Slot Grill Compact and Full Size?

The Compact is the Full Size at 80% scale: 146 vs 231 sq in of cooking area, 12-gauge vs 10-gauge carbon steel, and about 14 lb vs about 27.6 lb.

Which Slot Grill is best for camping?

The Compact suits one to three people and tight storage like trunks, kayaks and RVs. The Full Size suits families and groups traveling by truck, UTV or boat.

Can both sizes hold cast iron?

Slot Grill describes the Compact as strong enough for cast iron, and the Full Size offers more room for cast iron and big cuts.

How much do Slot Grills cost?
