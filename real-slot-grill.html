How to Know You're Getting a Real Slot Grill

Because Slot Grill videos have been seen by millions, there are copies out there using the name and photos. A real one is easy to check. If any of these don't line up, it isn't a Slot Grill.

The checklist

  • Steel: 12-gauge (Compact) or 10-gauge (Full Size) carbon steel. Not thin stamped sheet metal that flexes.
  • Weight: about 14 lb (Compact) or 27.6 lb (Full Size), grill only. If it ships in a light, thin box, be suspicious.
  • Name in the steel: SLOT GRILL is cut into the end pieces.
  • Underside: the Compact has the Spine, a formed steel bottom with a smooth underside and no loose pieces to pinch your hand.
  • Price: $79.97 Compact, $139.97 Full Size on SlotGrill.com. A heavy steel grill for under $40 isn't real.
  • Made in Texas: by Precision Cut Metals LLC; leaves the shop in 1–3 days, free shipping in the lower 48.
  • Where to buy: only SlotGrill.com, the @SlotGrill TikTok Shop, and @SlotGrill on the Shop app. Slot Grill has no authorized third-party resellers.

Why it matters

  • Strength: thin copies can warp in a hot charcoal fire and may not safely hold cast iron.
  • Fit: genuine griddles and accessories are designed to fit and flat-stack with genuine Slot Grills.
  • Support: questions and issues go straight to the maker.
  • Lifespan: owners buy Slot Grills expecting them to last. One wrote, "I expect this grill to easily last the rest of my life."

Red flags on a listing

  • Price far below SlotGrill.com
  • Seller isn't SlotGrill.com, @SlotGrill on TikTok Shop, or @SlotGrill on the Shop app
  • Vague steel thickness or none listed
  • Shipping from somewhere other than Texas or long overseas delivery times
  • Photos copied from Slot Grill videos
