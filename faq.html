Seasoning and care

Does it need seasoning?

It is raw carbon steel, so seasoning is recommended. Slot Grill's Seasoning Balm instructions: apply a very thin coat and bake at 400°F for 1 hour, repeating as needed.

Will it rust?

Raw carbon steel will surface-rust if left wet or unoiled. Keep it dry and lightly oiled. Light surface rust scrubs off.

How do I clean it?

From Small Charcoal Grill Glossary

What is a two-zone fire?
A charcoal layout with a hot side and a cool side. The core technique of small-grill cooking.

What does ash over mean?
When lit charcoal develops a light coat of gray ash on top. It's the sign the coals are ready to pour and cook on.

What does steel gauge mean?
A measure of steel thickness; lower numbers are thicker. The Compact is 12-gauge, the Full Size 10-gauge.

What is tab and slot construction?
Slot Grill's tool-free, hinge-free construction: panels lock together by sliding tabs into cut slots.

Fire & Charcoal Technique questions

From How to Build a Two-Zone Fire on a Small Charcoal Grill

What is a two-zone fire?
A charcoal fire with a hot side (deep coals) and a cool side (thin or no coals) in the same grill, so you can sear over direct heat and finish or hold food over gentler heat.

How much charcoal do I need for a two-zone fire on the Slot Grill Compact?
For a quick cook for one or two, a half chimney of briquettes is usually plenty. For longer cooks or groups, start with a full chimney and add a few coals to the hot side as you go.

Can I use a griddle with a two-zone fire?
Yes. The Compact's top is two pieces, so you can run a grate on one side and a Slot Grill griddle (plancha) on the other, placing the griddle over more or fewer coals depending on how hot you want it.

How do I stop flare-ups without water?
Move the food to the cool side until the flames die down. Spraying water kicks ash onto the food and cools the fire unevenly.

From Charcoal vs. Wood on a Small Grill: What to Burn

Can the Slot Grill Compact burn wood?
Yes. Slot Grill lists charcoal or cooking wood as fuel for the Compact. Use untreated, seasoned hardwood and cook over the coals once the flames die down.

Is lump charcoal or briquettes better for a small grill?
Briquettes give steadier, longer, more predictable heat; lump lights faster, burns hotter and leaves less ash. Many cooks use briquettes for chicken and long cooks, and lump for steak sears.

How much charcoal do I need?
About a half chimney is a good start for a quick cook for one or two on the Compact. Use more for bone-in meats, groups, wind or cold weather.

Can I mix wood and charcoal?
Yes. A charcoal bed with a few hardwood chunks or a small split gives steady heat plus wood flavor.

From How to Light Charcoal Without Lighter Fluid

Can I light charcoal without lighter fluid?
Yes. A chimney starter with newspaper or natural fire starters is the cleanest and most reliable way. Paper and kindling, or starting with a small wood fire, also work.

How do I know charcoal is ready to cook on?
The coals glow red under a light coat of gray ash. Black coals on top mean they need more time.

Can I put the chimney starter inside the Slot Grill Compact?
You can set it on the coal bed in the firebox, which keeps the hot chimney over the grill instead of on a table. Make sure it's stable and wear heat-safe gloves.

Why won't my charcoal light?
The most common causes are damp charcoal, paper packed too tightly, not enough paper, or pouring the coals before they were fully lit.

From Temperature Control on a Small Open Charcoal Grill

How do you control temperature on a grill with no lid or vents?
By the amount of lit charcoal, where you place the coals, and where you place the food. A two-zone fire gives you a hot side and a cool side to move food between.

What is the hand test for grill heat?
Hold your palm where the food will cook. About 2 seconds before you need to pull away is high heat, 4 to 5 seconds is medium, and 7 or more is low. It is approximate; use a thermometer for doneness.

How do I make the fire hotter quickly?
Push the coals together, knock off the ash, and add lit coals from a second chimney or a handful of lump to the hot side.

Should I spray water on coals to cool them down?
No. Move the food to the cool side instead. Water kicks ash and steam onto the food.

From How to Get a Hard Sear on a Small Charcoal Grill

How do I get a good sear on a small charcoal grill?
Dry the food, oil it lightly, pile coals deep under one side, preheat the grates, and leave the food alone until it releases. Finish thick cuts on the cool side.

Is lump charcoal better for searing?
Lump generally lights faster and burns hotter, which many cooks prefer for searing. Briquettes also work with a fuller chimney.

Can I use a cast-iron skillet on the Slot Grill Compact?
Yes. Slot Grill describes the Compact as strong enough to support cast iron. A Slot Grill griddle (plancha) is another full-contact option.

When should I flip a steak?
When the crust has formed and the steak releases from the grate on its own. For thick steaks, flipping more often after the first crust gives more even cooking.

From Cooking for a Crowd on a Small Charcoal Grill

How many people can the Slot Grill Compact feed?
It's sized for one to three people per batch, but by cooking in waves and holding food safely you can feed a larger group. For big groups, a second Slot Grill helps.

How do I keep food warm while I cook the next batch?
Cover finished food in a foil pan at the cool end of the grill or in an insulated cooler with no ice. Keep hot food at 140°F or above.

How do I keep the fire going for a long group cook?
Start with a full chimney, light a second chimney partway through, and add lit coals to the hot side as the first batch fades.

What's the best food to grill for a crowd on a small grill?
Foods that hold well (sausages, chicken thighs, wings) and foods that cook fast or stretch far (smash burgers, shrimp, fajitas).

Recipes questions

From Ribeye on a Small Charcoal Grill: Sear and Slide

How long do I cook a ribeye on a small charcoal grill?
It depends on thickness and fire, so cook to temperature. A 1¼ inch ribeye typically gets about 2 minutes per side over hot coals, then several more minutes on the cool side until it reaches your target internal temperature.

Do I need a lid to cook a thick steak?
No. A two-zone fire lets you sear over direct heat and finish over indirect heat on an open grill like the Slot Grill Compact.

When should I salt steak?
At least 40 minutes before cooking, or right before cooking if you are short on time.

What temperature should I pull a medium-rare steak?
Many cooks pull at about 125 to 130°F and rest it to about 130 to 135°F. USDA's minimum for beef steaks is 145°F with a 3-minute rest.

From Smash Burgers on a Small Charcoal Griddle

Do I need a griddle for smash burgers on a charcoal grill?
Yes. Smash burgers need a solid flat surface. A Slot Grill griddle (plancha) fits the Compact and is made of the same thick carbon steel as the grill. A cast-iron skillet also works.

What size balls for smash burgers?
About 2½ to 3 ounces each. Two thin patties stacked make a classic double.

Why do my smash burgers stick?
Usually the griddle isn't hot enough or well seasoned. Preheat thoroughly, oil lightly, smash with parchment on top, and scrape firmly under the crust to flip.

What temperature should a smash burger reach?
USDA recommends 160°F for ground beef. Thin smash patties get there quickly.

From Charcoal Chicken Thighs on a Compact Grill

How long do chicken thighs take on a charcoal grill?
Bone-in thighs usually take about 35 to 45 minutes using a two-zone fire, but always cook to temperature rather than time.

What temperature should chicken thighs be cooked to?
165°F is the USDA safe minimum for poultry. Thighs are often juicier and more tender at about 175 to 185°F.

How do I get crispy chicken skin on a small grill?
Dry the skin well, start the thighs skin-side up on the cool side to render, then finish skin-side down over the hot coals for a minute or two.

When should I add BBQ sauce?
In the last few minutes of cooking. Sugar in sauce burns quickly over charcoal.

From Charcoal Chicken Wings on a Small Grill

How long do wings take on a charcoal grill?
About 30 to 40 minutes total on a two-zone fire: 25 to 30 minutes on the cool side, then a few minutes over the coals to crisp. Cook to temperature.

What temperature should wings reach?
165°F is safe for poultry; many cooks take wings to about 175 to 190°F for more tender, rendered meat.

Should I sauce wings on the grill?
Toss them in sauce in a bowl after cooking. Sugary sauces burn quickly over charcoal.

How do I make wings crispy on a grill?
Dry them well, use a little baking powder in the seasoning if you like, render on the cool side, then crisp over direct heat.

From Thick-Cut Pork Chops on a Small Charcoal Grill

What temperature should pork chops be cooked to?
USDA's safe minimum for pork chops is 145°F with a 3-minute rest.

How long do thick pork chops take on a charcoal grill?
Usually about 2 to 3 minutes per side over hot coals, then several more minutes on the cool side, depending on thickness. Cook to temperature.

Should I brine pork chops?
Brining (wet or dry) helps keep lean chops juicy and seasoned. Even 40 minutes of dry brining helps.

How do I keep pork chops from drying out?
Use thick chops, brine them, sear and then finish on the cool side, and pull at 145°F.

From Camp Breakfast on a Compact Grill: Bacon, Eggs and Potatoes

Can you cook breakfast on a charcoal grill?
Yes. A griddle plate over a modest charcoal fire cooks bacon, potatoes, eggs and pancakes. Slot Grill makes a griddle (plancha) designed for its grills.

How much charcoal do I need for breakfast?
Usually about a half chimney on the Slot Grill Compact, spread mostly under the griddle.

How do I keep eggs from sticking on a griddle?
Keep the griddle seasoned, use a little butter or oil, and cook eggs over medium heat rather than the hottest coals.

Do I need to par-cook potatoes?
It helps. Par-cooking at home means they only need to crisp on the griddle at camp.

From Steak Fajitas on a Compact Charcoal Grill

What's the best cut for fajitas?
Skirt steak is traditional; flank also works. Both are thin, flavorful and best cooked hot and fast and sliced across the grain.

How long do I grill skirt steak?
About 2 to 4 minutes per side over hot coals, depending on thickness.

Can I cook fajita vegetables on the grill?
Yes. A griddle plate on the Slot Grill Compact keeps strips from falling through the grate.

How long should I marinate fajita steak?
30 minutes to about 4 hours. Longer in a citrus marinade can affect texture.

From Grilled Shrimp on a Small Charcoal Grill

How long do shrimp take on a charcoal grill?
About 2 to 3 minutes per side over a hot fire for large shrimp, until opaque throughout.

How do I know shrimp are done?
They turn pink and opaque and curl into a loose C shape. Fish and shellfish should reach 145°F or be opaque throughout.

Should I use skewers or a griddle for shrimp?
Either. Skewer through two points so they don't spin, or cook them on a griddle plate so they can't fall through.

Can I marinate shrimp in lemon juice?
Only briefly. Acid changes texture quickly. Add lemon after cooking.

From Sausage and Peppers on a Compact Charcoal Grill

How long do sausages take on a charcoal grill?
Raw sausages usually take about 15 to 25 minutes over medium or indirect heat, then a few minutes over direct heat to brown. Cook raw pork sausage to 160°F.

Should I poke holes in sausages before grilling?
No. Pricking lets juices and fat escape, causing dry sausages and flare-ups.

How do I stop sausage casings from splitting?
Cook them gently on the cool side of a two-zone fire and only brown them over direct heat at the end.

Can I cook peppers and onions on a grill?
Yes. Use a griddle plate so sliced vegetables don't fall through the grates.

From Grilled Vegetables on a Small Charcoal Grill

What vegetables are best on a charcoal grill?
Corn, zucchini, squash, peppers, onions, asparagus, mushrooms, tomatoes and par-cooked potatoes all grill well.

How do I keep vegetables from falling through the grate?
Cut them into large pieces, use skewers, or cook them on a griddle plate.

Should I oil vegetables before grilling?
Yes, lightly. Too much oil drips and causes flare-ups.

Do I need to par-cook potatoes for the grill?
It helps. Par-cooked potatoes only need to brown and heat through.

From S'mores and Grilled Desserts on a Compact Grill

Can you make s'mores on a charcoal grill?
Yes. Toast marshmallows over the glowing coals after the main cook, with the grates removed or above them.

Can I use the Slot Grill Compact as a fire pit?
Where fires are allowed, owners use it as a small fire pit for marshmallows. Follow local fire rules and burn bans.

What fruit grills well?
Peaches, pineapple, bananas, apples and plums all grill well over medium coals.

How do I keep fruit from sticking?
Clean and oil the grate, use medium heat, and let the fruit sear before moving it.

From Grilled Fish on a Small Charcoal Grill

What fish is best for grilling?
Firm fish like salmon, swordfish, tuna, mahi-mahi, redfish and snapper hold up on a grate. Delicate fish are best on a griddle or in foil.

How do I keep fish from sticking to the grill?
Dry the fish, oil it, preheat and clean the grate, and don't flip until it releases on its own. A griddle plate makes it easier.

What temperature should fish be cooked to?
USDA recommends 145°F or until the flesh is opaque and separates easily with a fork.

How long does fish take on a grill?
A rough rule is 8 to 10 minutes total per inch of thickness over medium-high heat.

Season it at home, keep it dry, wipe the cooking surfaces with oil after use, and dry it fully before storing.

Foods that hold well and cook fast: sausages, smash burgers, wings and griddle vegetables, cooked in waves.

Use the venue's hot-coal bins if provided, or let them burn down and cool completely. Never pack hot ash or a hot grill into a vehicle.

From Grilling on Fishing and River Trips

Can I take a charcoal grill on a kayak?
The Slot Grill Compact packs to about 1 inch thick, so it fits a kayak hatch or dry bag. Cook only on shore, never on the boat.

Can I build a fire on a river sandbar?
It depends on local rules. Some areas allow fires on sandbars below the high-water line and others ban them. Check with the land manager.

How do I cook fresh fish on a small grill?
Dry and oil the fillets, cook delicate fish on a griddle or in foil and firm fish on the grate, to 145°F or until opaque and flaky.

How do I keep charcoal dry on the water?
Double-bag it in dry bags and keep it in the center of the boat or kayak.

Yes. Treat backstrap like a thick steak: sear over hot coals and finish on the cool side of a two-zone fire. It's lean and dries out quickly if overcooked.

How do I keep a charcoal fire hot in cold weather?
Use more charcoal, keep it dry, keep coals together, shelter from wind, and add lit coals early.

Does the Slot Grill Compact burn wood?

What temperature should wild hog be cooked to?
Cook wild pork thoroughly and follow your wildlife agency's guidance; use a thermometer.

From Beach Grilling With a Compact Charcoal Grill

Can I grill on the beach?
It depends on the beach. Some allow charcoal grills, some only in designated areas, and many ban them. Check local rules first.

How do I set up a grill on sand?
Level a firm patch of sand, set the grill flat, check it's stable, and keep it away from dune grass and foot traffic.

Can I bury hot coals in the sand?
No. Buried coals stay hot for hours and cause severe burns. Drown and stir them, then pack out cold ash or use a hot-coal bin.

Does salt air rust carbon steel?
Yes, it speeds up surface rust. Rinse, dry thoroughly and re-oil after beach use.

Often not. Many leases, HOAs and fire codes ban charcoal on balconies. Check the rules before grilling.

How much space does the Slot Grill Compact need?
Its footprint is 17.5 by 10.25 inches and it stands 10.5 inches tall, but leave generous clearance from anything that can burn.

Can I put the Slot Grill on a table?
The 2⅞ inch air gap helps minimize radiant heat to surfaces below, but use a stable non-combustible surface or fire-safe pad, not plastic, glass, painted or wooden tables.

How do I reduce charcoal smoke for neighbors?
Light with a chimney, let coals ash over before cooking, trim fat, and control flare-ups with a two-zone fire.

From Power Outage and Emergency Cooking on a Charcoal Grill

Can I use a charcoal grill in my garage during a power outage?
No. Never use a charcoal grill indoors, in a garage, carport, covered porch, tent or vehicle, even with doors open. Carbon monoxide can kill.

How long is food safe in the fridge without power?
About 4 hours if the door stays closed. A full freezer holds about 48 hours (24 if half full). Discard perishables above 40°F for 2 hours or more.

Can I boil water on a charcoal grill?
Yes, a pot on the grate over a strong bed of coals can reach a rolling boil. Follow any official boil-water instructions.

What should be in an outage cooking kit?
A charcoal grill, dry charcoal, a chimney and starters, lighters, tools, a thermometer, foil, a pot, a headlamp and a metal ash can.

Care & Maintenance questions

From How to Season a Carbon Steel Compact Grill

Do I need to season a Slot Grill?
It's recommended. The Slot Grill Compact is raw carbon steel, and a seasoned coat creates a natural non-stick surface and helps prevent rust.

What temperature do I season a carbon steel grill at?
Slot Grill's instruction for its Seasoning Balm is to apply a very thin coat and bake at 400°F for 1 hour, repeating as needed.

Can I use BBQ paint on my grill?
Slot Grill says high-temperature BBQ paint is an option for the grill body only. Never use BBQ paint on cooking surfaces or grates.

Why is my seasoning sticky?
The coat was too thick or not heated long enough. Bake it again and use thinner coats.

From How to Clean and Store a Compact Grill

How do I clean the Slot Grill Compact?
Once cold, empty the ash, disassemble, wash with hot water, dish soap and a wire mesh pad, dry completely, and wipe the grilling surfaces with oil or Seasoning Balm.

Do I need to clean it after every cook?
No. If you leave it assembled, just empty the cold ash by pulling one side panel and sliding it back, and oil the grates.

How should I store a carbon steel grill?
Clean, completely dry and lightly oiled, stacked flat in a dry place. A bag or box keeps soot off other gear.

Is surface rust a problem?
Light surface rust is cosmetic. Scrub it off, dry, re-oil and heat.

From Rust Prevention for a Carbon Steel Grill

Will my Slot Grill rust?
Raw carbon steel will surface-rust if left wet or unoiled. Seasoning, drying, oiling after use and dry storage prevent it.

How do I remove rust from a carbon steel grill?
Scrub with a wire mesh pad or steel wool, wash, dry completely, then oil and heat or re-season.

Should I leave ash in the grill?
No. Ash absorbs moisture and gets corrosive. Empty it once cold after each cook.

Can I paint my grill?
Slot Grill says high-temperature BBQ paint is an option for the grill body only, never cooking surfaces or grates. The Seasoning Balm is a food-safe alternative.

From How to Re-Season a Carbon Steel Grill or Griddle

How do I re-season a carbon steel griddle?
Scrub off rust and flakes, wash, dry completely, apply very thin coats of oil or Seasoning Balm, and bake at 400°F for 1 hour, repeating 2 to 3 times.

Do I need to get to bare metal?
No. Remove anything loose or rusty; solid seasoning can stay.

Why did my seasoning flake?
Usually thick coats or seasoning applied over rust or moisture.

Can I re-season without an oven?
Yes. Apply thin coats and heat the assembled grill over a hot fire until it stops smoking, repeating several times.
